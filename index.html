01 / About

Any career is a green career.

Many of us care about climate change but feel stuck, because we only see a few kinds of solutions. This hackathon is where that changes.

Teams of 3 to 5 graduate and undergraduate students from every field take on real Tufts challenges, learn from workshops and mentors, and turn ideas into working solutions. Beginners are welcome, and if you don’t have a group, don’t worry: students without a team will form groups on the day of the event.

02 / The build

How it works.

Use workshops and makerspaces to turn your idea into a working solution.

Submit by 3:30 pm Sunday and present to the judges.

04 / Prizes and judging

Build to win.

1st place$500
2nd place$375
3rd place$250

Prize money is subject to change, with the amounts expected to increase. It is split within your team. Winners get the option of turning their idea into a Green Fund project, with direct help filling out the Green Fund application.

Round 1: initial review

Judges review projects and recordings and choose the finalists.

Round 2: finalists

Finalists present to a panel for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place. Judging criteria will be announced.
