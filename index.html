01 / About

Any career is a green career.

Many of us care about climate change but feel stuck, because we only see a few kinds of solutions. This hackathon is where that changes.

Teams of 3 to 5 students from every field take on real Tufts challenges, learn from workshops and mentors, and turn ideas into working solutions. Beginners are welcome.

05 / Prizes and judging

Build to win.

1st place$500
2nd place$375
3rd place$250

Prize money is split within your team. Winners also get help turning their idea into a Green Fund application. Continuing the project is optional.

Round 1: by track

Each track's judges review projects and recordings and choose one finalist per track.

Round 2: finalists

Finalists present to a panel for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place. Judging criteria will be announced.
